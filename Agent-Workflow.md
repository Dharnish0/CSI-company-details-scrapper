# Corporate Research Agent Workflow

## Step 1

Open the root company website.

## Step 2

Identify pages related to:

- Operating Groups
- Companies
- Portfolio
- Acquisitions
- Businesses
- Subsidiaries

## Step 3

Extract potential entities.

## Step 4

Classify each entity.

## Step 5

Identify the official website.

## Step 6

Verify the parent-child relationship.

## Step 7

Store the entity.

## Step 8

Add the entity to the research queue.

## Step 9

Visit the entity's website.

## Step 10

Look for additional corporate entities.

## Step 11

Repeat recursively.

## Step 12

Stop when no additional relevant entities are discovered.

## Step 13

Deduplicate entities.

## Step 14

Validate relationships.

## Step 15

Generate Excel output.
